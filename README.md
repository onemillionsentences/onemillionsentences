# One Million Sentences

**One book. One million sentences. Your words. Forever.**

A collaborative literary project where anyone can contribute one original English sentence to a shared book.

## How It Works

1. **Write** — Submit one original English sentence
2. **Review** — Your sentence is manually reviewed within 48 hours
3. **Pay** — Once approved, pay $5 to secure your place
4. **Published** — Your name and sentence number appear in the book permanently

## Key Facts

- Each sentence costs **$5** to publish
- One person can contribute **multiple sentences** (each $5)
- The goal is **1,000,000 approved sentences**
- The goal is a target, not a guarantee
- Full refund if your sentence is rejected
- Every 5 confirmed paid referrals = 1 free sentence slot

## Rules

- English only
- Original writing only (no copying)
- No religious, political, adult, or offensive content
- Between 10–300 characters

## Tech Stack

- **Hosting**: GitHub Pages
- **Database**: Supabase
- **Payments**: Paddle
- **Email**: Brevo
